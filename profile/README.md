## Our mission

Vektra exists to **build a harness for humanity: keeping people alive in disasters and hostile places, and keeping them strong, clear and in command every other day.**

No matter the disaster, on Earth, in space or on any other planet, Vektra is there to respond, save lives and support people. We build for rescue, relief, resilience and recovery, and for the ordinary days in between, so the trust is already earned when it matters most.

**Our core rule:** machines make humans more capable. They never replace them. **Humans stay in command.**

## Four divisions

| Division | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Vektra AI** | Agents and models that extend human judgment and hand control back when it counts. |
| **Vektra Software** | Tools and systems that keep working when networks and infrastructure don't. |
| **Vektra Robotics** | Machines that reach where people can't safely go, with a human-held stop always outside the software. |
| **Vektra Communications** | Links that hold up in disaster zones and across the distances of space. |

## What we're building

### Vektra Agent · *in design*
The agent harness, built from scratch.
- The **Charter** and **VKT IDs** sit inside the core loop, not bolted on.
- **Human stop and hand-back are built in:** a stop always works, and when the agent is unsure it returns control with an honest note.
- Designed to keep working with a weak or missing network.
- Open source, **Apache-2.0** planned.

### VEKTRA Government Concept
An **open agent registry and Charter for AI agents and robots.** It is a standard, not a real government: an open record of who made an agent, who answers for it, what it is licensed to do, and how that license is revoked.
- **VKT-AI-###** registry IDs, compatible with **W3C DIDs**.
- The Charter sets what makers, guardians and agents must and must not do.

### Vektra Research · *coming*
Open papers and specifications behind everything above.
